Hello! I am Amber

About Myself

I was born in America after my family moved here. When I was a baby, I lost my hearing. I attended Milwaukee School for Sign Language during elementary and middle school, then moved to a school for Deaf students when I was 14.

My Hometown

At home, we often feed wild birds when they visit. I have a garden with roses, and I enjoy taking care of them. I love spending peaceful time outside and watching the animals I see every day.

My Interests and Hobbies

I like expressing how I feel through painting, and I enjoy being creative. I love video games because they are fun and challenging. I enjoy spending time with my birds because they are smart, playful, and sometimes a little crazy! I also like playing volleyball with my cousin and friends.
